## Featured projects

### 📊 Data Cleaning and Preparation — stock market data
Cleaned and prepared a real stock dataset: merged 10,597 historical prices with 6,460 stock listings, handled missing values, removed outliers with the IQR method, fixed invalid values, and built features (7-day and 30-day moving averages, daily returns, 7-day future close) ready for machine learning. Python, pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn.

→ [`BIA-AI-Assignments/Project 2.ipynb`](https://github.com/ayobellobia/BIA-AI-Assignments/blob/main/Project%202.ipynb)

### 🤖 Unsupervised learning — handwritten digit clustering
Explored the MNIST digits dataset (1,797 samples), normalized the data, visualized it, and grouped the digits with KMeans clustering, PCA and t-SNE. Python, scikit-learn, matplotlib.

→ [`BIA-AI-Assignments/Project4.ipynb`](https://github.com/ayobellobia/BIA-AI-Assignments/blob/main/Project4.ipynb)

### 🚗 NYC Motor Vehicle Collisions dashboard (Power BI)
A two-page dashboard on 238,421 New York City motor vehicle collisions (NYPD open data, January 2021 to April 2023): 10 KPI cards for cyclists, motorists, pedestrians and total persons injured and killed, a borough slicer, monthly trend analysis, collisions by day of week and hour of day, top streets, vehicle types and contributing factors — with written insights on each page.

→ [`NYC-collisions-dashboard.pbix`](https://github.com/ayobellobia/Portfolio/blob/main/NYC-collisions-dashboard.pbix) (open in Power BI Desktop)

### ☕ Coffee Brand Dashboard (Tableau)
A dashboard on 1,245 global coffee brands (Coffee Review data): roast origins, roaster locations, types of roast, price per 100g and ratings — with a pie chart, price vs rating, top roasters, a world map of coffee origins and a year breakdown.

→ [`Coffee-brand-dashboard.twbx`](https://github.com/ayobellobia/Portfolio/blob/main/Coffee-brand-dashboard.twbx) (open in Tableau)
